# PhD Research Alignment

This repository extends the centralized cross-domain `trustworthy-digital-twin-cps-benchmark` into distributed collaborative intelligence.

Core research directions:

1. robust federated learning under heterogeneous CPS data;
2. digital-twin-informed client trust;
3. attack-versus-fault-versus-domain-shift diagnosis;
4. asynchronous/stale-update robustness;
5. privacy/utility/robustness interaction;
6. cross-domain transfer and personalization;
7. explainable trust decisions.

The intended dissertation contribution is not merely a new aggregation formula. It is an experimental methodology for asking whether cyber-physical evidence improves federated robustness without systematically excluding benign but unusual clients.
