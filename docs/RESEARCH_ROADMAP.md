# Research Roadmap

## v0.2 — rigorous diagnosis
- multi-seed confidence intervals;
- multiclass confusion matrices;
- calibrated probabilistic client diagnosis;
- history-aware recovery after transient faults/attacks;
- geometry-only vs twin-only vs fused trust ablations.

## v0.3 — richer learning
- small neural models and representation learning;
- personalization and clustered federation;
- leave-one-domain-out federated transfer;
- unknown-attack evaluation.

## v0.4 — formal robustness/privacy
- clearly specified Byzantine assumptions;
- validated robust aggregation baselines;
- formal differential-privacy accountant if appropriate;
- sensitivity analysis.

## v0.5 — higher-fidelity domain adapters
- adapters to validated simulators or measured datasets where licensing and safety allow;
- explicit model-mismatch studies;
- systems-level communication emulation.
