# Frozen v0.1 Baseline

The narrative baseline and caveats are maintained in [`docs/BASELINE_FINDINGS.md`](../docs/BASELINE_FINDINGS.md).

Frozen comparison point: five seeds (`11,22,33,44,55`), 30% scaling attackers, 21 clients, 12 rounds. Mean global MSE: FedAvg 0.004691; median 0.000904; trimmed mean 0.000604; trust-weighted 0.002690; TwinTrust 0.001443. TwinTrust malicious acceptance averaged 0.033 with zero benign rejection in this simplified baseline.
